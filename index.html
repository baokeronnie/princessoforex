Meet The Founder

Built by a trader, run as a business.

Princess of Forex was founded in 2019 to make forex education and market access practical for everyday traders in Botswana and across the region. What started as one trader sharing signals with friends has grown into a structured mentorship, signals, and community brand with a recognisable identity and an engaged following.

Today the brand combines three revenue lines — lifetime signals, one-on-one mentorship, and virtual coaching — alongside an active broker referral partnership and a loyal, vocal client base. It's a lean, founder-led operation with clear room to professionalise further under new ownership.
